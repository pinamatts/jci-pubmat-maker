Before you use them
  • Use white logos on dark or photo backgrounds, and the colour or black ones on light backgrounds.
  • Don't stretch, recolour or redraw a logo. Leave space around it.
  • On pubmats, logos sit top left. The 2027 line-up order is JCI Philippines · JCI Cebu · Champion Genuine Impact | El Vinta · SALAMAT.
  • The old blue flame “79” logo and the 2026 DARE logos are retired. Don't use them on anything new.
